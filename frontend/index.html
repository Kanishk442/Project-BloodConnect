Services

Everything a donation programme needs

From the first appointment to the lab result, BloodConnect keeps donors and hospital teams on the same page.

AI-assisted blood analysis

Classify white blood cell types and screen smear images for malaria, leukemia (ALL) and sickle cell indicators.

Appointment management

Donors book a slot with a hospital; staff confirm, reschedule or complete it from their dashboard.

Hospital workspace

Record donations, upload samples and keep a searchable history of every test performed.

Instant alerts

Donors are notified when a screening needs attention so follow-up care is never delayed.

Donor recognition

Milestone badges celebrate repeat donors, from a first donation to platinum status.

Find hospitals

Browse registered hospitals and choose the one closest to you when booking.

How it works

Four simple steps

1

Create an account

Register as a donor or as a hospital in under a minute.

2

Book or manage

Donors pick a hospital and time; hospitals review requests.

3

Donate & screen

Staff record the donation and can upload smear images for AI-assisted screening.

4

Follow up

Results and alerts appear on the donor dashboard for timely care.
